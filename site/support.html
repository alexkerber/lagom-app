What to put in a report

  • Your device and system version, for example "iPhone, iOS 26.1" or "Windows 11".
  • The lagom version, if you know it. On a phone, find it in the system settings under Apps, then lagom.
  • Your mail provider: Gmail, iCloud, Outlook, Fastmail or another.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot helps. Cover any private mail first.

Common questions

How do I add a Gmail account?

On macOS, Windows and Linux, type your address. lagom opens Google sign-in in your browser. Sign in there and come back. If you prefer a password, choose "Use an app password instead".

On iPhone, iPad and Android, use an app password. Google sign-in is not in the phone apps yet.

Google needs an app password here, not your normal password. You need 2-Step Verification on your Google account to make one. See Google's help on app passwords.

How do I add an iCloud account?

iCloud Mail needs an app-specific password, not your Apple Account password. Make one at account.apple.com under Sign-In and Security. See Apple's help.

How do I add an Outlook or Microsoft 365 account?

On macOS, Windows and Linux, type your address. lagom opens Microsoft sign-in in your browser. Sign in there and come back.

Microsoft sign-in is not in the phone apps yet. Some Microsoft accounts work there with an app password. Others need sign-in, so use the desktop app for now.

For a work or school account, your IT admin may have to allow lagom, or IMAP, first. If sign-in asks for admin approval, send them this page.

My provider is not listed

Type your address and password. lagom tries to find the server settings. If it cannot, it asks you for them. Your provider's help pages list the IMAP and SMTP servers. If they offer app passwords, use one.

Notifications do not arrive

On macOS, Windows and Linux, turn on "Notifications for new mail" in lagom Settings. Also allow notifications for lagom in your system settings.

On a phone, allow notifications for lagom in your system settings. lagom checks for new mail itself, in the background, when the system lets it. Android does this about every 15 minutes. iOS decides when, so a notification can come late. lagom has no push server, so your mail never goes through us.

Where is my mail stored?

On your device and on your mail server. lagom has no server of its own. The privacy page has the details.

How do I remove an account?

  • macOS, Windows and Linux: open Settings and choose "Remove account".
  • iPhone: tap the mailbox name at the top and choose "Remove" with the address.
  • iPad: touch and hold the account under Accounts in the sidebar, then choose "Remove account".
  • Android: open the Mailboxes menu and choose "Remove" with the address.

lagom then deletes that account's mail and password from the device. Its tasks stay as local tasks. Your mail on the server stays as it is.

Which calendars work?

On macOS, Windows and Linux: Google Calendar and Google Tasks, for Google accounts that sign in with Google. lagom asks for this access only when you allow calendar. The phone apps do not sync calendars or tasks yet.
