# Three flat icon directions

- **1 — Vikningen:** A single folded L connects the name Lagom to paper and a calm place for incoming work.
- **2 — Samlat:** Three streams join into one clear direction: mail, meetings and tasks gathered in one place.
- **3 — Andrum:** A soft, solid form surrounds an open slot: room for what matters, without filling every space.

All marks were drawn from scratch as SVG paths. These are concept studies, not platform exports. Each uses an 824 px rounded tile on a transparent 1024 px canvas. The board shows actual 256, 128 and 32 px image sizes against light and dark Dock-like backgrounds.

Rebuild with `./render.sh` from this directory (requires `rsvg-convert`). No GUI, downloaded artwork, fonts embedded in the marks, or image-generation service is used.

## Reference study

The references informed hierarchy, silhouette and small-size testing; their marks and artwork are not included in these assets.

- [Things OS 26 icon](https://culturedcode.com/things/blog/): a readable central symbol and consistent tile footprint.
- [Mimestream](https://mimestream.com/): a recognizable mail silhouette with a restrained palette.
- [Craft OS 26](https://www.craft.do/os26): a compact geometric mark whose parts remain legible at small sizes.
- [Linear icon and interface update](https://linear.app/changelog/2024-03-20-new-linear-ui): solid shape and negative space carry recognition.
- [Apple app icon guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons): platform context for icon shape and presentation.

The resulting design choices are our own interpretation of these references.
