lagom is Swedish for "not too much, not too little, just right".

lagom mail gets you to an empty inbox, then gets out of the way. Your mail, what is due today and your lists, in one calm app.

Every message gets one answer:
• Done: it leaves the inbox.
• Reply: write back and move on.
• Later: pick a time, and it comes back then.

Swipe right for Done and left for Later. The list tells you how many are left, so you know when you are finished.

Today shows your day in one sentence: how much is unread, what is due and what is next. Tap a number to go there.

One inbox for all your accounts, or one account at a time. Later, Done, Sent, Drafts, Trash and your own folders are always close. Search finds mail in every account. Move a message to a folder, and undo if you change your mind.

Write in plain text or Markdown. Drafts save as you type, signatures are per account, and Undo send gives you a moment to take it back.

To-do keeps simple lists on your phone. Add a task from a message, and it remembers where it came from.

Your mail goes from your phone to your own mail server and nowhere else. lagom has no account of its own, no ads, no tracking and no AI features. Remote images stay off until you tap "Load remote images", so senders cannot see when you read. Your password is encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Works with IMAP accounts: Gmail and iCloud with an app password, Fastmail and most other providers. Type your address and lagom finds the server settings.

A notification tells you when new mail arrives. Light and dark follow your system. A sidebar on tablets, a tab bar on phones.

lagom is open source under the MIT licence. Made in Sweden.
