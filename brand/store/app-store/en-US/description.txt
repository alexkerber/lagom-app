lagom (LAH-gom) is Swedish for not too much, not too little. Just right.

lagom mail gets you to an empty inbox, then gets out of the way. Your mail, what is due today and your lists, in one calm app on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

THREE ANSWERS
Every message gets one: Done, Reply or Later. Done clears it. Later brings it back when you have time. The list says how many are left, so you know when you are finished. On iPhone, swipe right for Done and left for Later.

TODAY
Your day in one sentence: how much is unread, what is due and what is next. Tap a number to go there.

ONE INBOX
All your accounts in one list, or one at a time. Later, Done, Sent, Drafts, Trash and your own folders are always close. Search finds mail in every account. Move a message to a folder, and undo if you change your mind.

WRITING
Reply, Reply all and Forward. Plain text or Markdown. Drafts save as you type, signatures are per account, and Undo send gives you a moment to take it back.

TODO
Simple lists that live on your device. Add a task from a message, and it remembers where it came from.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your mail goes from your device to your own mail server and nowhere else. lagom has no account, no tracking, no ads and no AI inside. Remote images stay off until you load them, so senders cannot see when you read. Passwords stay in the Keychain.

WORKS WITH YOUR MAIL
Any IMAP account. Type your address and lagom finds the settings. Gmail and iCloud work with an app-specific password.

MADE FOR EACH SCREEN
A sidebar on iPad and Mac, tabs on iPhone, light and dark. On Mac: keyboard first, with a command palette for everything.
